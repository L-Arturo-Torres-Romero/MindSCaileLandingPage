This system is a **Human-in-the-Loop AI system designed to support the generation and refinement of safety requirements for critical systems and software**.

Rather than automatically generating and approving requirements, the system places the **engineer at the center of the decision-making process**. The AI analyzes the available system information, use cases, hazards, and safety-related inputs, and provides suggestions for safety requirements. The engineer can then **accept, reject, or modify each suggestion**, maintaining human control over the final requirements.

The system incorporates a **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** architecture to improve the relevance and consistency of the AI-generated suggestions. Relevant information from engineering knowledge bases, safety practices, standards, and previously defined requirements can be retrieved and provided as context to the AI before generating its suggestions.

A **KPI-based evaluation system** is also integrated to measure the effectiveness and efficiency of the solution. The evaluation can track metrics such as **suggestion acceptance rate, rejection and modification rates, requirement generation time, and other indicators of human-AI interaction and engineering efficiency**. These metrics provide feedback on how effectively the system supports engineers and help identify opportunities for continuous improvement.

The overall approach combines **Artificial Intelligence, RAG, Systems Engineering, Requirements Engineering, Human-in-the-Loop interaction, and Functional Safety practices**, with particular relevance to standards such as **ISO 26262** and **SOTIF (ISO 21448)**.

The objective is not to replace engineering expertise, but to **augment engineers with AI-assisted recommendations while preserving human judgment, review, and accountability throughout the requirements engineering process**.
