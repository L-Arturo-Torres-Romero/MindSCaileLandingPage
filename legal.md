## 2. Cookie Policy

### 2.1 What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. They are used to ensure core functionality, remember preferences, and analyze site performance.

### 2.2 Cookies Used on This Website
- **Strictly Necessary Cookies:** Required for core site security, navigation, and remembering your cookie consent choice.
- **Google Analytics Cookies:** First-party cookies used to distinguish unique users and measure aggregated website traffic and session statistics.
- **Microsoft Clarity Cookies:** Cookies used to generate heatmaps and anonymized session replays to evaluate usability and page performance.

### 2.3 Managing Cookies
You can accept or reject non-essential analytics cookies through our Cookie Consent Banner upon visiting the site, adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time, or enable the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal in your browser, which our website respects for U.S. opt-out preferences.
